{% trans "Evidence-based health news and knowledge for Nepal." %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}Much of the health information people in Nepal see online has never been checked against the evidence, while important research stays locked in technical language. {{ JOURNAL_NAME }} exists to close that gap: we turn medical evidence into accurate, clear and practical information, without lowering the standards of journalism, research ethics or professional accountability.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}We publish health news, explainers, research summaries and analysis in Nepali and English, alongside a peer-reviewed scientific journal. Formal submission and peer review for the journal take place on OJS (Open Journal Systems), a dedicated scholarly publishing platform; accepted papers are published here with plain-language summaries.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "OUR VISION" %}

{% trans "To become Nepal's most trusted evidence-based health and medical knowledge platform, advancing medical education, healthcare, science communication and public health awareness through research-driven information, credible journalism and academic publishing." %}

{% trans "OUR MISSION" %}

{% trans "To reduce misinformation in healthcare and medical education by turning scientific evidence, health research and medical knowledge into accurate, accessible content for the Nepali public, health professionals, students, researchers and policymakers." %}
